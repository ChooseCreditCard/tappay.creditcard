Contactless payments explained

TapPay.creditcard breaks down how payments work without inserting or swiping cards:

  • NFC chip in the card or device
  • Tokenized card number for security
  • Short-range radio (~4 cm)
  • Network authorization in milliseconds

Use-cases: card, phone, wearable

Security & best practices

Secure tap & mobile wallet usage:

  • Use device biometrics or PIN
  • Enable alerts for each transaction
  • Remove unused cards from your wallet
  • Erase device remotely if lost

Which cards work best?

For the best tap and mobile wallet performance, look for cards with:

  • No foreign-transaction fees
  • High rewards for wallet usage
  • Strong virtual card support
  • Global NFC compatibility
