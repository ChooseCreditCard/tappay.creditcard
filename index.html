Contactless payments explained

TapPay.creditcard shows how payments work without swiping or inserting a card:

  • • NFC chip in the card/device
  • • Tokenised card number for security
  • • Near-field communication (~4 cm range)
  • • Payment networks handle authorization instantly

Use-cases: card, phone, wearable

Contactless card

Traditional plastic card with NFC — tap at checkout.

Mobile wallet

Apple Pay, Google Pay — tokenised virtual card on your phone.

Wearable

Watch, ring or band — ultra-fast checkout, transit ready.

Security & best practices

Secure usage of tap & mobile wallet:

  • • Use biometrics or PIN on device
  • • Enable transaction alerts
  • • Remove unused cards from your wallet
  • • Lock or erase your device if lost

Which cards work best?

To benefit fully from contactless and mobile wallets, look for:

  • • Cards with no foreign-transaction fees
  • • Cards with strong rewards on mobile wallet usage
  • • Cards with integrated wallet/virtual card support
  • • Cards with global acceptance and NFC support
